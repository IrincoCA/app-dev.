#  My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man: No Way Home

##  Overview
*Spider-Man: No Way Home* is a superhero movie from the Marvel Cinematic Universe. It follows Peter Parker as he deals with the consequences of his identity being revealed to the world.

##  Why I Like It
- Emotional storyline  
- Action-packed scenes  
- Nostalgic characters from previous Spider-Man movies  
- Strong message about responsibility  

##  Main Characters
1. Peter Parker / Spider-Man  
2. Doctor Strange  
3. Green Goblin  
4. Doctor Octopus  

## 💬 Favorite Quote
> "With great power comes great responsibility."
